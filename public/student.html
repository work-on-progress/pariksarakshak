What happens when you press start
  1. Your browser asks Open Safe Exam Browser? — choose Open.
  2. Safe Exam Browser starts and locks the machine.
  3. You are signed in automatically. No password to type again.
  4. The paper you chose opens. Read the rules, then begin.

Safe Exam Browser must already be installed on this machine. If nothing happens when you press start, tell the invigilator.
